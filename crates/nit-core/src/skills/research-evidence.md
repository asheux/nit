# Compare research evidence

Frame the assigned question as a choice the downstream team needs to make. Name the population, workload, version or operating conditions that affect that choice. Read upstream findings first and identify the remaining uncertainty.

## Trace the evidence

For each candidate, trace its strongest claim to an original paper, dataset, official documentation or observed implementation. Record the reference and the relevant section, version or date. Identify which claims are independently supported and which repeat a common source. When sources disagree, compare their definitions, inputs and evaluation conditions.

## Rank the strategies

Rank viable strategies by the operator's stated criteria. For each, give the supporting evidence, a tradeoff and the condition that could change the ranking. Distinguish a direct observation from an inference. “Only measured on small inputs” tells the next investigator which evidence is missing.

## Hand off the findings

Use the requested Sources, Methods, Assumptions and Ranked strategies sections. A survey hands these to its dependants. A writer persists the assigned portion of the judge's content map in its own file and supplies the path for the master index.
